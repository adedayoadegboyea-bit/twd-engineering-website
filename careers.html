HOW APPLICATIONS WILL WORK

A structured process from application to interview.

The planned portal keeps applicants informed while giving management a central record of every application.

01
Submit applicationApplicant provides contact details, qualifications, experience, role and application letter, with CV/supporting documents.
02
Application receivedApplicant receives a confirmation email and management receives a new-application notification.
03
Review & shortlistManagement updates the application status from received to under review or shortlisted.
04
Interview scheduledInterview date, time, location and format are entered by management and a confirmation is sent to the applicant.
05
Final decisionSuccessful, not-selected and other status updates can be communicated directly from the recruitment system.
