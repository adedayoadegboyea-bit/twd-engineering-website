Project documents & automated BOQ request

Submit drawings and site reports. TW&D prepares the quantities and pricing; clients do not enter quantities or rates.

Site layout: Upload the registered land survey under “Land survey”. Enter the plot width/depth and setbacks from that survey. The concept site plan will show the building footprint within the plot boundary; a survey drawing is not automatically interpreted as legally verified coordinates until reviewed by TW&D.
Required for a dependable quotation: architectural drawings, site survey and levels, and a geotechnical soil investigation report. Structural drawings/design are required before reinforcement and foundation quantities can be treated as final. An initial review may identify missing information.

Save your project brief first. Files are private to your account and TW&D review staff.

After your documents are submitted, TW&D will review the design inputs, verify measurement information, and prepare a traceable BOQ for professional approval. No material quantities or rates are entered by the client.
