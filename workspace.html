Welcome to the TW&D Workspace.

Access your building design brief, project records, estimates, supplier quotations and engineering workflows from one secure online entry point. Use your own account; your access depends on your assigned role.

How everyone gets access

1. Open the workspace linkUse a phone, tablet or computer with an internet connection and a modern browser.
2. Create an individual accountRegister with your own email and confirm it if email confirmation is enabled. Never share login credentials.
3. TW&D assigns the right roleClients see their own records; suppliers submit quotations; authorised staff receive pricing or administrative permissions.

Note: The 3D viewer currently produces a conceptual massing preview and GLB/footprint exports. It is not yet a complete architectural CAD/BIM production system or a structural-analysis engine.
