ENGINEERING • REGULATORY

COREN

Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria. The official portal provides practitioner and firm services, registration, renewals, verification and other engineering regulatory functions.

The NSE provides professional membership, engineering resources, publications, technical activities, training and industry engagement for engineers in Nigeria.
