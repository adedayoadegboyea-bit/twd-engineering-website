From project conception to completed development.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR • FOUNDER & CORPORATE EXECUTIVE

Engr. Adedayo Abiola Adegboye

2014Building-project rootsNOVEMBER 2017TW&D establishedRC 1730471Registered company identity

Engr. Adedayo Abiola Adegboye is the founder and corporate executive of TW&D Engineering Consult & Services Ltd. The company profile presents him as an engineering and construction leader with extensive experience in the building industry and a strong focus on disciplined project implementation.

The profile records that the company evolved from solo building projects beginning in 2014. As demand for housing and infrastructure grew, the need arose for an integrated project-management function; by November 2017, TW&D Engineering Consult & Services Ltd was established to bring key multi-disciplinary contributors together under one roof — from project conception, design and sourcing through infrastructure, professional-team coordination, construction and final handover.

His leadership profile is closely connected with the company's delivery philosophy: competent personnel, quality management, organised execution, site supervision, safety, environmental responsibility and practical coordination of civil, electrical, mechanical and building services. The profile also describes TW&D's ability to pursue projects within planned deadlines and budgets while responding to project challenges.

“Engineering leadership is not only about putting structures on the ground; it is about bringing people, planning, technical knowledge and responsible execution together to create lasting value.”
