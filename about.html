ABOUT TW&D

Engineering, construction and development.

TW&D Engineering Consult & Services Ltd is an Ibadan-based engineering and construction company providing coordinated engineering, construction, consultancy and property development services.

The company was established in November 2017 and has developed its work through building projects, civil works, technical services and property-related activities.

Our approach is practical: understand the requirement, coordinate the technical work and deliver with attention to quality, safety and durability.

CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Engr. Adedayo Abiola Adegboye

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Engr. Adedayo Abiola Adegboye provides the leadership and strategic direction behind TW&D Engineering Consult & Services Ltd, an Ibadan-based company committed to dependable engineering, construction, technical consultancy and property development services.

His leadership philosophy places particular emphasis on understanding each client's objectives, approaching projects with sound technical judgement, and coordinating the people and resources needed to deliver work with care and accountability. From initial consultations and planning to site coordination and project delivery, he champions clear communication, practical solutions and attention to lasting value.

Under his direction, TW&D continues to build its reputation around professionalism, client relationships and a commitment to quality, safety and durability. His vision is to develop a company that clients can approach with confidence—whether they require engineering advice, construction services, property solutions or specialist technical support.

Leadership commitment: “We design and we build to last.”
